Print settings

The safe area keeps figures and their weapons clear of the base edge, and export normally refuses a base that crosses it. Turning it off prints the base as laid out: the warning stays, but PDF and PNG export no longer stop. Anything past the base edge is cropped, and cut lines run along that edge.

    Calibrate screen size

    Hold a bank card against the screen and drag the slider until the bar is exactly as wide as the card (85.6 mm). Screens differ, so each browser remembers its own setting. Recalibrate if you change the browser zoom.

    Importing a force

    1. Build your force in the Midgard force builder ↗.
    2. Open its Settings tab and choose Download your forces. This saves a forces.json file.
    3. Here, click Import force and choose that file. It holds every force in the builder's library, so you'll be asked which one to import.

    Each stack becomes a troop template with one base per copy, and each hero a leader. The builder has no artwork, so check each template's artwork afterwards. Importing the same force again merges the changes and keeps the artwork and formations you have set.
