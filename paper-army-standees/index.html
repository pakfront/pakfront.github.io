Print options

Base labels

Edit names below each unit. Blank names use the artwork name. Separate labels are 38 × 4 mm (heroes 18 × 4 mm). Wraparound mode adds 10 mm tabs: 3 mm down the base edge and 7 mm underneath. Foot units have names on both end tabs; mounted units have front/rear side tabs and separate front/back name wraps. Edge text is 6 pt; test with your base.

02 Build the fold

Set each Foot or Cavalry unit’s type beside its base quantity. Warriors and Skirmishers use their own row counts; Heavy and Light Riders use their own riders across. Cavalry and mounted heroes print mirrored profiles on both faces. Mounted heroes always use one upright. Cavalry folds run along the selected base depth. Monstrosities deepen their ground sections while keeping fixed uprights; heroes keep their 20 × 20 mm bases.

Panel height includes weapons and 1 mm total clear space. Infantry artwork fills its panel; cavalry profiles keep their proportions. Monsters keep their proportions: fixed 35 mm panel for the Giant and 23 mm for Trolls and Ogres. All feet stay at the panel bottom.

03 Color the setup

Infantry uses small shadows on both sides. Cavalry and mounted heroes cast a larger, compressed silhouette to one side, filling the available ground toward the next rider or base edge.

One palette for the whole setup. Ground color fills every flat foot; solid color or gradient fills both faces, including blank backs. Gradients run from the bottom color at the feet to the top color at the crest. Rear silhouette color applies to infantry silhouette backs. Colors are saved with your setup.

04 Set up the sheet

Outer cut outlines and a 40 mm scale check always print. Keep printer scaling at 100% / actual size.
