THE PAPER ARMY WORKSHOP

Paper Army Standees.

Small armies. Real dimensions.
40 × 20 mm units · 20 × 20 mm heroes

From warband to tabletop.

Choose your units, set the folds, and print a sheet of miniature ranks.

01 Choose your units

0 bases

Edit names below each unit. Blank names use the artwork name. Cut nameplates separately and glue them onto your base; check the fit on your chosen base.

Each copy makes one base. Ranks on a base repeat the selected artwork. Mounted heroes use one upright on a 20 × 20 mm base. The Giant uses one 35 mm upright; Trolls and Ogres each have two figures on one 23 mm upright. All use 40 × 20 mm bases.

02 Build the fold

Cavalry and mounted heroes print mirrored profiles on both faces. Mounted heroes always use one upright. Its folds run along the 20 mm short edge.

Panel height includes weapons and 1 mm total clear space. Infantry artwork fills its panel; cavalry profiles keep their proportions. Monsters keep their proportions: fixed 35 mm panel for the Giant and 23 mm for Trolls and Ogres. All feet stay at the panel bottom.

03 Color the setup

One palette for the whole setup. Ground color fills every flat foot; figure color fills both faces, including blank backs. Rear silhouette color applies to infantry silhouette backs. Colors are saved with your setup.

04 Set up the sheet

Outer cut outlines and a 40 mm scale check always print. Keep printer scaling at 100% / actual size.
